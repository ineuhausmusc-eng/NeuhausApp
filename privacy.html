Legal & Support

Support & Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Prayer Matters is designed to be a quiet and private place for prayer, Scripture, reflection, and journaling. Your journal entries and Bible notes are stored on your device. Limited technical information is processed to deliver updates, measure update adoption, stream audio, and keep the app reliable, as explained below. The app does not display ads or use your data for advertising tracking.

Support

For help with Prayer Matters, contact Neuhaus Apps at support@neuhausapps.com .

When reporting a problem, please include the app name, your device model, your operating system version, and a brief description of the issue.

Please do not include private prayers, journal entries, personal reflections, or other sensitive information in a support email unless you intentionally choose to share that information.

Overview

Prayer Matters is developed by Neuhaus Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how the Prayer Matters iOS and Android apps and their support website handle information.

Prayer Matters keeps the journal entries, written reflections, and Bible annotations you create locally on your device. The app does not provide an account or a service that uploads or syncs that content to Neuhaus Apps. App-update and audio-delivery providers process limited technical information when those services are used.

Information We Collect

Prayer Matters and its service providers process limited technical update activity, a random installation identifier, crash reports, and other update diagnostics. Audio and website requests also involve ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and the requested file. See the sections below for the purposes and providers.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising is displayed.
  • Limited technical update analytics are used to measure update adoption.
  • No advertising tracking across other apps or websites is used.
  • No advertising profile is created.
  • No personal information is sold or shared for advertising.
  • No payment-card information is collected by Neuhaus Apps.

If you voluntarily contact Neuhaus Apps by email, Neuhaus Apps will receive the email address, message, and information you choose to provide. That information will be used only to respond to your request, provide support, or maintain necessary support records.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you create or save in Prayer Matters remains locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Private journal entries, written prayers, and reflections
  • Gratitude and answered-prayer entries
  • Bible notes, highlights, and underlines
  • Daily Walk with God marks and streak history
  • Reading, display, and other app preferences

Neuhaus Apps does not receive, view, store, or have remote access to this information.

Prayer, Bible, and Faith-Based Content

Prayer Matters includes Christian prayer, Scripture, Bible-reading, devotional, audio, and faith-based content.

Your written prayers, journal entries, Bible annotations, and personal reflections are not uploaded by the app to Neuhaus Apps or its update service. Streaming a selected audio-guided prayer requires requesting that recording from the audio provider. Prayer Matters does not use your religious activity to create an advertising or marketing profile.

Audio and Microphone Access

Audio-guided prayers are streamed from Cloudflare R2 and require an internet connection. The provider receives the requested recording and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and technical request metadata, to deliver and protect the service.

Prayer Matters does not record your voice, listen through your microphone, or request microphone permission. Playing a prayer does not upload a voice recording, journal entry, or Bible note. Written prayers, the Bible, and saved journal content can be read on the device without streaming audio.

App Updates and Diagnostics

Prayer Matters uses Expo EAS Update to check for and download compatible app updates. Update requests include technical information such as the operating system, app and runtime version, project and update identifiers, recent failed-update identifiers, and a random installation identifier. Network providers also receive connection information needed to handle the request.

The installation identifier is generated randomly on the device. It is not derived from a hardware identifier or an account, but it stays consistent across app launches and updates so update activity can be associated with that installation. It supports update delivery, installation and adoption measurements, and troubleshooting.

After certain fatal app errors, the update library can send a limited error report with a later update request. Crash and other update diagnostics help identify failed updates and maintain reliability. The app does not upload your journal entries or Bible notes to this service. This technical processing is not used for advertising tracking or to personalize ads.

Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

Prayer Matters uses limited technical update analytics and diagnostics as described above. It does not use advertising tracking or combine activity across other companies’ apps or websites to target or measure advertisements.

Prayer Matters does not:

  • Use behavioral or targeted advertising
  • Display advertisements
  • Track activity across other apps or websites
  • Sell personal information
  • Share personal information for advertising purposes

Because Prayer Matters does not engage in tracking, its iOS version does not request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency system.

Purchases and App Stores

Prayer Matters is distributed through Apple’s App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. The relevant store independently processes purchases, downloads, updates, refunds, payment information, and other store activity under its own terms and privacy practices.

Neuhaus Apps does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information through Prayer Matters.

Device Backups

Neuhaus Apps does not provide a Prayer Matters account, separate cloud storage, or cloud-sync service.

Depending on your platform, device, and settings, locally stored Prayer Matters information may be included in operating-system backups or device transfers. On iOS, these may include iCloud or computer backups. On Android, Google or your device manufacturer may provide backup or transfer services. These services are controlled by those providers and your device settings, not by Neuhaus Apps.

Because locally stored information may include private journal entries or written prayers, users should review their device backup settings when storing sensitive information.

Data Security

Prayer Matters is designed to keep user-created content locally on the user’s device. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device, device passcode, Apple or Google account, backups, and device settings.

Users should consider protecting their devices with a passcode, an available biometric lock, or both when storing private prayers, journal entries, or personal reflections.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Neuhaus Apps does not collect or possess your private app content, Neuhaus Apps cannot retrieve, restore, or remotely delete that information.

You may remove individual information using deletion controls available within Prayer Matters. Deleting Prayer Matters from your device may also delete information stored locally by the app, subject to backups or transfers maintained by your operating-system or device provider.

Neuhaus Apps does not provide an account-recovery or cloud-based content-recovery service for locally stored Prayer Matters information.

Technical service records are handled under the relevant providers’ retention and deletion policies for service operation, reliability, security, and applicable legal requirements. Deleting local app data does not itself guarantee deletion of records already held by a provider or information in a device backup. Contact Neuhaus Apps with questions or requests about technical data or support correspondence; see the provider privacy links below for their practices.

Children’s Privacy

Prayer Matters does not ask children to create accounts or provide private information. The limited technical processing described in this policy can occur when the app is used.

Parents and guardians should determine whether the app’s Bible, prayer, devotional, and faith-based content is appropriate for their children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Neuhaus Apps or have a privacy concern, please contact us.

Third-Party Services

Expo EAS Update: Expo processes installation identifiers, update activity, and diagnostic information for app update delivery, adoption measurement, and reliability. See Expo’s Privacy Policy and its privacy explanation.

Cloudflare R2: Cloudflare delivers streamed prayer audio and processes technical network requests needed to provide and protect that service. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Prayer Matters is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple may independently process information associated with purchases, downloads, updates, refunds, device diagnostics, backups, and App Store services according to Apple’s Privacy Policy .

On Android, Google Play independently processes information associated with app purchases, downloads, updates, refunds, and store services. Android backup or device-transfer services may also process information according to your device and account settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the privacy information supplied by your device manufacturer.

This support and privacy website may be hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information necessary to deliver, operate, protect, and secure the website according to GitHub’s Privacy Statement .

This website uses Google Fonts to display its typography. When the website loads, a visitor’s browser may connect to Google’s servers to retrieve those font files. Google may receive limited technical information associated with that request according to Google’s Privacy Policy .

These website services do not provide Neuhaus Apps with access to your private Prayer Matters journal entries, written prayers, Bible notes, or other locally stored app content.

Your Choices and Rights

You control the information stored locally within Prayer Matters. You can delete saved information using available in-app controls or remove the app and its locally stored information from your device.

You can manage device backups, privacy settings, and other system-level choices through your device’s Settings app.

If you have contacted Neuhaus Apps by email and would like Neuhaus Apps to delete information contained in that support correspondence, you may send a request to support@neuhausapps.com .

External Links

Prayer Matters or this support website may provide links to websites operated by Apple, Google, Neuhaus Apps, or other parties.

Neuhaus Apps is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independently operated third-party websites. Users should review the privacy policy of any external website they choose to visit.

Changes to This Policy

Neuhaus Apps may update this Privacy Policy if Prayer Matters’ features, services, or privacy practices change.

When this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date will be changed.
