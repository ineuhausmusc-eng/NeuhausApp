This Privacy Policy explains how Ian Neuhaus, the individual developer and operator of Neuhaus Apps (“Neuhaus Apps,” “I,” “me,” or “my”), handles information in connection with NeuhausApps.com and applications published under the Neuhaus Apps name.

Prayer Matters is currently the only application offered by Neuhaus Apps.

01 / 17

Privacy Overview

Prayer Matters is designed to provide a private space for prayer, Scripture reading, journaling, reflection, gratitude, and spiritual growth.

Prayer Matters does not require users to create an account. Neuhaus Apps does not operate a cloud-storage system for users’ prayers, journal entries, reflections, gratitude entries, streak information, or other private content.

This content is stored locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to, received by, or accessible to Neuhaus Apps.

Neuhaus Apps does not:

  • Sell personal information.
  • Use personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Display third-party advertisements.
  • Send prayer, journal, reflection, or gratitude content to an artificial intelligence service.
  • Operate social, messaging, or user-to-user sharing features.
  • Use third-party analytics inside Prayer Matters.
  • Request access to the user’s microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or location.
02 / 17

Information Stored by Prayer Matters

Prayer Matters may allow users to create or save information such as:

  • Prayers.
  • Journal entries.
  • Reflections.
  • Gratitude entries.
  • Prayer history.
  • Streak and progress information.
  • App preferences.

This information is stored within the application’s local storage on the user’s device. Neuhaus Apps cannot view, retrieve, or receive this information.

Prayer Matters does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or cloud storage.

03 / 17

Scripture and Audio Content

Scripture used for features such as Verse of the Day is selected from content bundled within Prayer Matters. The app does not send a request to an external Bible or verse-of-the-day service when displaying this content.

Prayer and background audio included with Prayer Matters is bundled with or otherwise supplied as part of the application. Private user content is not transmitted to an audio-hosting service.

04 / 17

iCloud Backups

Although Neuhaus Apps does not provide cloud storage, Apple may include locally stored Prayer Matters data in an ordinary iCloud device backup if the user has enabled iCloud Backup.

These backups are controlled by Apple and the user. Neuhaus Apps cannot access, retrieve, modify, or delete information contained in a user’s iCloud account or iCloud backups.

Users can manage their iCloud backup settings through their Apple device settings.

05 / 17

App Store and Apple Services

Prayer Matters is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple may process information related to downloading or purchasing the app, including transaction, account, device, diagnostic, and payment information.

Neuhaus Apps does not receive users’ complete payment-card information. Apple handles App Store transactions according to Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

Depending on a user’s Apple privacy and diagnostic-sharing settings, Apple may provide Neuhaus Apps with aggregated sales information or limited technical and crash information. Such information does not include the contents of users’ prayers, journals, reflections, or gratitude entries.

06 / 17

Website Information

NeuhausApps.com does not currently use an advertising network, contact form, or developer-installed analytics service.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When someone visits a GitHub Pages website, GitHub may log technical information such as the visitor’s Internet Protocol address for security and service-operation purposes. GitHub handles this information according to its own privacy statement.

Neuhaus Apps does not use the website to obtain access to Prayer Matters content stored on users’ devices.

07 / 17

Email Communications

If a person contacts Neuhaus Apps by email, Neuhaus Apps will receive the information the person voluntarily provides, such as:

  • The sender’s email address.
  • The sender’s name, if provided.
  • The contents of the message.
  • Attachments or technical information included by the sender.

This information will be used to respond to the inquiry, provide support, address legal or safety concerns, and maintain appropriate records.

Users should not include private prayers, journal entries, medical information, or other sensitive personal information in support emails unless necessary.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of Neuhaus Apps and its users.

08 / 17

Cookies and Tracking

Neuhaus Apps does not currently place its own advertising or analytics cookies on NeuhausApps.com.

GitHub or other infrastructure providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver, secure, and operate their services. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Neuhaus Apps does not use cross-site tracking or behavioral advertising.

09 / 17

How Information Is Shared

Neuhaus Apps does not sell or rent personal information.

Information voluntarily provided to Neuhaus Apps may be disclosed only:

  • To service providers needed to operate email, website hosting, or application distribution.
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users, Neuhaus Apps, or others.
  • In connection with a transfer of the Neuhaus Apps applications or related assets, subject to applicable privacy obligations.

Private content stored only within Prayer Matters cannot be shared by Neuhaus Apps because Neuhaus Apps does not receive or have access to that content.

10 / 17

Data Retention and Deletion

Private Prayer Matters content remains on the user’s device until the user deletes it through available app controls or deletes the application and its related data.

Deleting Prayer Matters from an iPhone removes the app and its locally stored related data. Offloading the app may preserve its documents and data so they remain available if the app is reinstalled.

Copies contained in an Apple-controlled iCloud backup may remain until the user modifies or deletes the applicable backup through Apple’s settings.

Neuhaus Apps cannot remotely retrieve or delete locally stored application data or data contained in a user’s Apple account.

To request deletion of information voluntarily sent directly to Neuhaus Apps, such as a support email, contact support@neuhausapps.com. Certain records may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping purposes.

11 / 17

Children’s Privacy

Prayer Matters is a general-audience faith-based application intended for users age 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Neuhaus Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because Prayer Matters does not require accounts and private content remains locally stored, Neuhaus Apps does not receive children’s prayers, journals, or reflections.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child under 13 has sent personal information directly to Neuhaus Apps, the parent or guardian may contact support@neuhausapps.com to request its deletion.

12 / 17

Data Security

Neuhaus Apps uses reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the limited information it handles.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices, Apple accounts, passcodes, backups, and email accounts.

13 / 17

International Users

Prayer Matters may be made available in countries and regions where Apple permits its distribution.

Neuhaus Apps is operated from Florida, United States. Information voluntarily sent to Neuhaus Apps, such as support emails, may be processed in the United States or in other locations where Apple, GitHub, or applicable service providers operate.

By contacting Neuhaus Apps, users acknowledge that their information may be processed outside their country of residence, subject to applicable legal protections.

14 / 17

Privacy Rights

Depending on where a person resides, applicable law may provide rights regarding personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how personal information is handled.

Because Neuhaus Apps does not receive private content stored within Prayer Matters, Neuhaus Apps cannot access, correct, export, or remotely delete that locally stored content.

Requests concerning information provided directly to Neuhaus Apps may be sent to support@neuhausapps.com. Neuhaus Apps may need to verify the requester’s identity before completing a request.

15 / 17

Third-Party Services

Neuhaus Apps currently relies on third-party services including:

  • Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, optional diagnostics, and user-controlled iCloud backups.
  • GitHub for website hosting and related security and operational services.
  • Apple iCloud Mail for email services associated with the neuhausapps.com domain.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Neuhaus Apps does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

16 / 17

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Neuhaus Apps changes its applications, website, service providers, or legal obligations.

The updated policy will be posted on NeuhausApps.com with a revised “Last Updated” date. Material changes will be communicated through the website, the application, or another appropriate method when required by law.
