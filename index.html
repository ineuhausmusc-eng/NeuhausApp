Support

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Where can I download the apps?

OrderLess, Prayer Matters, and SwiftShift Job Compare are on the App Store for iPhone. Each card in the collection links straight to its listing.

Are the apps available on Android?

Not yet. Android versions are in the works, and Google Play links will be posted on the Android updates page as each app is released.

What does SwiftShift Job Compare do?

It helps you compare job offers side by side, including pay, paid time off, and commute costs. It is available now on the App Store.

How do I get help with an app?

Email Support@neuhausapps.com with the app name and what you need. SwiftShift also has a support page. Privacy policies and terms are linked on each app card and in the footer.
