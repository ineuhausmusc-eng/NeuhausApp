Support

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Where can I download the apps?

SwiftShift Job Compare, OrderLess, and Prayer Matters are on the App Store for iPhone. Each card in the collection links straight to its listing.

Are the apps available on Android?

Yes. Prayer Matters is available on Google Play. SwiftShift Job Compare and OrderLess are currently available for iPhone. Each app’s download buttons are in the collection.

What does SwiftShift Job Compare do?

It helps you compare job offers side by side, including pay, paid time off, and commute costs. It is available now on the App Store.

How do I get help with an app?

Email Support@neuhausapps.com with the app name and what you need. SwiftShift also has a support page. Privacy policies and terms are linked on each app card and in the app policy directory.
