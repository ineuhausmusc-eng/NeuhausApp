Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

SwiftShift Job Compare is developed by Neuhaus Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how SwiftShift handles information.

SwiftShift’s core job comparison works on your device without a SwiftShift account. When these features are available in your version, online answers, email drafting, subscriptions and optional ad measurement handle information as described below.

Information We Collect

If you choose to send an online question, SwiftShift shows what will be shared. Your question and a summary of both offers, including employer and role names, pay, schedule, time off and commute details, go through our Supabase service to OpenAI to generate an answer. The online request includes text and excludes audio. SwiftShift requests that OpenAI disable response storage. Supabase processes technical request information to run and secure the service. OpenAI may process or retain information under its API data policies.

If you choose to polish a recruiter email with Ink Flow, SwiftShift sends through Supabase to OpenAI the email's purpose and tone, the requested pay or deadline when applicable, and any questions or notes you choose to include. Your recipient, signature, employer names from your comparison, and the rest of the comparison stay on your device. Free text you enter in the questions or notes is included. SwiftShift opens your email app when you choose; you review and send the draft there.

Apple handles Pro billing and payment details. SwiftShift uses signed Apple transaction information to verify access. The online service keeps records of subscription revocations and billing grace periods for entitlement checks. It also uses a daily keyed hash of your network address to limit repeated requests and removes older daily usage records when processing a new request. If you contact support, we use your email address and the details you send to respond.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into SwiftShift, such as job titles, employer names, pay, schedules, paid time off, benefits, commute details, notes, or comparison results, is stored on your device. You decide when to share a comparison by PDF, spreadsheet, email or copy. The app or service you select handles the shared information under its own privacy practices.

You can delete a saved comparison in the app. To ask about access to or deletion of information sent to support or stored by the service, contact us at the address below.

Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

Some iOS builds include Meta’s SDK for measuring which Facebook or Instagram ads lead to an install or app opening. In builds configured for this measurement, SwiftShift activates the SDK after you grant iOS tracking permission. Meta can receive the device advertising identifier, app activation events and related technical information. SwiftShift’s Meta integration excludes your questions, audio and offer details.

You can decline tracking and still use SwiftShift. To change your choice on iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. SwiftShift does not show ads inside the app. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

iCloud and Device Backups

Neuhaus Apps does not provide cloud storage or cloud sync for SwiftShift. Apple may include locally stored app data in normal iCloud or device backups if those features are enabled on your device. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not Neuhaus Apps.

Children's Privacy

SwiftShift is a job comparison tool. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the app, contact us using the address below.

Third-Party Services

SwiftShift is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may process information related to downloads, purchases, device diagnostics, and App Store services according to Apple's own privacy policies.

SwiftShift checks Expo for eligible app updates. Expo processes the app's update request, including version and platform information, and may collect installation identifiers, network addresses and update or launch diagnostics. SwiftShift's update request excludes your saved comparisons, questions and drafts. See Expo's update data disclosure.

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the website according to GitHub's own privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

If SwiftShift's privacy practices change in the future, Neuhaus Apps will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

Contact
