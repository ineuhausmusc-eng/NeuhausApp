SwiftShift Support & Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Support

For help with SwiftShift Job Compare, contact Neuhaus Apps at support@neuhausapps.com.

If you are reporting a bug, please include the app name, your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Privacy Policy

SwiftShift Job Compare is developed by Neuhaus Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how SwiftShift handles information.

SwiftShift is designed to work locally on your device. Neuhaus Apps does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information through SwiftShift.

Information We Collect

SwiftShift does not collect any data.

The app does not require an account, does not ask users to sign in, does not use advertising, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into SwiftShift, such as job titles, employer names, pay, schedules, paid time off, benefits, commute details, notes, or comparison results, stays on your device.

Neuhaus Apps does not receive, view, store, or have access to this information.

Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

  • SwiftShift does not track users.
  • SwiftShift does not use third-party analytics.
  • SwiftShift does not show advertisements.
  • SwiftShift does not sell or share user data.

iCloud and Device Backups

Neuhaus Apps does not provide cloud storage or cloud sync for SwiftShift. Apple may include locally stored app data in normal iCloud or device backups if those features are enabled on your device. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not Neuhaus Apps.

Children's Privacy

SwiftShift has an App Store age rating of 4+. Neuhaus Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children because SwiftShift does not collect personal information from anyone.

Third-Party Services

SwiftShift is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may process information related to downloads, purchases, device diagnostics, and App Store services according to Apple's own privacy policies.

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the website according to GitHub's own privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

If SwiftShift's privacy practices change in the future, Neuhaus Apps will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact Neuhaus Apps at support@neuhausapps.com.
