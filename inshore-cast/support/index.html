About Inshore Cast

Inshore Cast provides local fishing forecasts, species-specific Bite Scores, tide and weather context, Cast Alerts, Live Cast, and optional Inshore Cast Pro features for inshore anglers.

Forecasts and alerts are estimates and should always be checked against official weather, tide, safety, and fishing-regulation sources.
