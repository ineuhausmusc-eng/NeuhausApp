Privacy Policy

How DOT Physical: Examiner Guide handles account, subscription, question, and technical information.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

No patient profiles. The app is not designed to store driver medical records, and Ask conversation history is not saved as an in-app record. Do not enter protected health information.

Overview

DOT Physical: Examiner Guide is developed by Neuhaus Apps as a professional reference and decision-support product for certified medical examiners. This policy applies to the iOS app and its related support pages.

The app is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FMCSA or the U.S. Department of Transportation.

Information Used to Provide the Service

  • Account information: when you choose Apple or Google sign-in, the authentication provider and Supabase may process an account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image made available by that provider.
  • Subscription information: Apple and RevenueCat may process product identifiers, purchase status, transaction information, trial eligibility, and an app user identifier needed to provide subscription access.
  • Questions submitted to Ask: the text you intentionally submit may be sent securely to the app’s protected backend and an AI service provider to generate a response and perform accuracy checks. Do not include patient-identifying or protected health information.
  • Security and technical information: the app and its service providers may process app version, device or operating-system information, request timestamps, integrity signals, IP address, and limited diagnostic information needed to secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Support correspondence: Neuhaus Apps receives the information you voluntarily include when contacting support.

Information the App Is Not Designed to Collect

DOT Physical: Examiner Guide does not create patient profiles and is not an electronic health record. It is not designed to collect driver names, dates of birth, certificate numbers, medical-record images, or other protected health information.

Ask conversation history is not saved as an in-app record. Limited server logs may temporarily contain request metadata or submitted text when necessary for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, or legal compliance. Users should submit de-identified professional questions only.

How Information Is Used

  • Authenticate users and protect subscription access.
  • Provide source-linked reference, search, and Ask features.
  • Check responses against approved official-source content and deterministic safeguards.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, verify app integrity, and maintain service reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reported errors.

Neuhaus Apps does not sell personal information and does not use the app for third-party targeted advertising.

Service Providers

The app may rely on Apple, Google, Supabase, RevenueCat, Expo/EAS, OpenAI or another configured AI provider, and hosting or infrastructure vendors. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy and only for the functions for which it is used.

Official-Source Monitoring

The service may retrieve and compare publicly available materials from official government websites, including FMCSA and eCFR sources, to identify potential updates. This process concerns public source documents and is not intended to collect patient information.

Retention and Account Requests

Account, entitlement, security, and diagnostic information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide and protect the service, satisfy legal requirements, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may differ across service providers.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information controlled by Neuhaus Apps, contact support@neuhausapps.com. Some purchase and transaction records remain controlled by Apple or must be retained where legally required.

Security

Neuhaus Apps uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access and protected server-side credentials. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

The app is a professional tool for certified medical examiners and is not directed to children.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to support@neuhausapps.com.
