Help & Contact

Support

Help with sign-in, subscriptions, source-linked content, or application behavior.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Contact Neuhaus Apps

Email support@neuhausapps.com. Please include “DOT Physical: Examiner Guide,” your device model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description of the issue.

Do not include driver names, dates of birth, certificate numbers, medical records, photographs of medical forms, or other protected health information.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions and free trials are billed through Apple. You can manage or cancel an active subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases inside the app if an existing entitlement is not recognized.

Neuhaus Apps cannot view your full payment-card number and cannot directly cancel or refund an App Store transaction. Apple handles billing and refund decisions under its policies.

Clinical or Source Corrections

If you believe a citation, source status, or regulatory summary needs correction, email support with the page title, source citation, and the issue you observed. Do not send patient-specific facts.

Official regulations and current FMCSA materials control. Qualification decisions remain the responsibility of the certified medical examiner.

Account Help

The app supports Apple and Google sign-in. For account-access problems, tell us which provider you used, but never send your password, one-time code, or authentication token.
