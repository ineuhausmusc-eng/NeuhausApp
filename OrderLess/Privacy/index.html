01 / Overview

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how the OrderLess: Takeout Tracker iOS application ("OrderLess" or the "App") and this OrderLess support and privacy webpage handle information. OrderLess is developed and operated by Ian Neuhaus, an individual developer operating under the Neuhaus Apps name ("Neuhaus Apps," "we," "us," or "our") in Florida, United States.

OrderLess is an accountless tool that helps users reduce restaurant delivery and pickup orders through a gradual weekly plan. No Neuhaus Apps login or account is required.

In plain language: we cannot remotely see your OrderLess plan, individual orders, spending estimates, or reflections because the App stores that content locally on your device.
02 / Local app data

Information stored on your device

OrderLess stores the following information in a local SQLite database on your device when you choose to provide or create it:

Onboarding and plan An optional first name; who you usually order for; prior attempts; obstacles; ordering frequency; baseline orders; estimated weekly spending; target orders; plan duration; motivations; and challenges.
Order history Locally generated record identifiers, dates and times, delivery or pickup selection, optional order prices, week association, and chosen currency label.
Reflections A day rating, selected emotions or patterns, an optional note, and the date associated with the reflection.
Preferences Currency label, haptic and reduced-motion choices, notification settings and times, local notification identifiers, timezone, and app-state flags.

These records are used on your device to calculate weekly allowances, estimated savings, patterns, progress, and reminder timing. Neuhaus Apps does not operate a server that receives this content and cannot view, retrieve, recover, or remotely delete it.

OrderLess does not connect to a bank, credit-card account, restaurant account, delivery service, email inbox, contacts list, location history, HealthKit, camera, microphone, or photo library. An optional order price or weekly spending estimate is a value you enter manually; it is not imported from a financial institution.

Locally generated record identifiers are used to organize records within the App. They are not advertising identifiers and are not used to track you across apps or websites.

03 / Paid access

Purchases, Apple, and RevenueCat

OrderLess offers a recurring weekly subscription and a separate lifetime purchase. Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Neuhaus Apps does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

OrderLess uses RevenueCat, Inc. to obtain product offerings, check introductory-offer eligibility, validate purchases, determine entitlement status, restore purchases, help prevent purchase fraud, and provide purchase-performance analytics.

Because OrderLess does not have an account system and does not provide a custom user identifier to RevenueCat, the RevenueCat SDK generates an anonymous App User ID. RevenueCat also processes purchase and subscription information associated with that identifier, such as product and transaction information, receipt and entitlement status, renewal or expiration status, and related App Store purchase events. Neuhaus Apps does not send your name, email address, OrderLess plan, individual order history, spending entries, or reflections to RevenueCat.

Purchase history is used for app functionality and purchase analytics. OrderLess does not use RevenueCat for advertising and does not configure RevenueCat with your email address, phone number, or an advertising identifier.

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

04 / Reminders

Local notifications

OrderLess asks for notification permission only after showing an explanatory screen. If you allow notifications, the App can schedule daily reminders, a Monday allowance-reset reminder, and—when enough local order history supports it—an optional pattern-based reminder.

These notifications are scheduled locally on your device. OrderLess does not request an Expo push token, does not use a Neuhaus Apps push-notification server, and does not send your order history to create reminder timing. Notification permission status, settings, local scheduling identifiers, and timezone are stored on your device.

You can turn reminders off within OrderLess and can change or revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

05 / Portability

Optional data export

OrderLess includes an Export my data control. If you choose it, the App creates a JSON file containing your onboarding answers, plan versions, orders, reflections, preferences, and export timestamp in the App’s temporary cache and opens Apple’s share sheet.

Nothing is exported unless you initiate this action and select a destination. Neuhaus Apps does not automatically receive the export. Any person, app, cloud service, or other destination you choose may receive and retain the file under its own privacy practices. You are responsible for choosing a secure destination, especially if the file contains a name, spending estimates, or private reflections.

The temporary cached copy may remain until it is replaced, removed by the operating system, removed when local app data is cleared, or deleted with the App. Copies you send elsewhere must be deleted from those destinations separately.

06 / Support

Support communications

If you voluntarily contact Neuhaus Apps, we receive the contact information and message content you choose to provide, such as your email address, device model, iOS version, screenshots, anonymous purchase identifier, or problem description. We use support information to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, maintain necessary support records, and comply with legal obligations.

Please do not include detailed spending history, private reflection notes, another person’s information, payment-card details, passwords, or other sensitive information in a support message unless it is necessary and you intentionally choose to provide it.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the support request, security, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or legal compliance, then deleted or de-identified when reasonably practicable.

08 / No tracking

Tracking, advertising, sale, and sharing

OrderLess does not display advertisements, use advertising SDKs, build advertising profiles, or track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework because OrderLess does not engage in tracking as Apple defines that term.

Neuhaus Apps does not sell personal information. Neuhaus Apps also does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising. RevenueCat purchase analytics are used to operate and understand paid access, not to advertise to you across unrelated services.

09 / Apple services

iCloud backups and device transfers

Neuhaus Apps does not provide an OrderLess cloud account, cloud-storage service, or multi-device sync service. Apple may include locally stored OrderLess data in an iCloud backup, encrypted computer backup, or device-transfer process depending on your Apple and device settings.

Those Apple-controlled copies are not accessible to Neuhaus Apps. Deleting information inside OrderLess may not immediately remove an older copy retained in an existing Apple backup. Review and manage backups through your Apple settings.

10 / Data lifecycle

Retention and deletion

Local OrderLess data

Your local App data remains on your device until you delete individual records, use Delete all data in OrderLess Settings, or delete the App, subject to any Apple-managed backup or transferred copy. The in-app deletion control removes onboarding answers, plan versions, orders, reflections, preferences, and app flags from OrderLess’s local database and cancels its scheduled local reminders.

Purchase information

Deleting local OrderLess data or deleting the App does not cancel an App Store subscription, delete Apple’s transaction record, or necessarily erase the RevenueCat purchase record. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase information under their applicable policies and as needed for transaction history, restoration, fraud prevention, accounting, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Restoring a purchase may re-establish entitlement information.

Support information

You may request deletion of information you voluntarily sent to Neuhaus Apps. We will honor verified requests when required and reasonably possible, subject to records we must retain for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal claims, or other legal obligations.

11 / Your control

Your choices and privacy rights

You can:

  • Review, correct, export, or delete locally stored information using OrderLess controls.
  • Turn reminders off in the App or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Manage or cancel a renewing subscription through your Apple account settings.
  • Choose not to send a data export or support message.
  • Ask Neuhaus Apps to access, correct, delete, restrict, or provide a copy of personal information we possess, where applicable law grants that right.
  • Object to or withdraw consent from applicable processing and appeal a denied privacy request where local law provides those rights.

Because Neuhaus Apps cannot access your locally stored App content, we cannot remotely provide, correct, or delete that content for you. For a request involving RevenueCat data, contact Neuhaus Apps so we can help route or process the request. We may need information reasonably necessary to locate the correct anonymous purchase record and verify the request.

We do not discriminate against users for exercising applicable privacy rights. To submit a request, email support@neuhausapps.com with the subject “OrderLess Privacy Request.”

12 / Minors

Children’s privacy

OrderLess is a general-audience habit and budgeting-support application and is not directed to children under 13. Neuhaus Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through OrderLess. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Neuhaus Apps through a support communication, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

13 / Safeguards

Data security

OrderLess reduces privacy risk by keeping habit, spending, order, and reflection records on the device and by avoiding accounts, advertising, tracking, and developer-operated cloud storage for that content.

Purchase communications with Apple and RevenueCat use their secured systems. Nevertheless, no device, transmission, storage method, or service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You can help protect local data by using a device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, keeping iOS current, protecting your Apple account, and choosing secure destinations for exported files.
